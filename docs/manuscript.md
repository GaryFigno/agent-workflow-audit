# Companion manuscript

Wang Jiayi, Independent Researcher, China.

**Evidence Retention and Resource Accounting in Multiagent Game Development: A Retrospective Study of a Shared Engine Workflow**

[Read the manuscript](https://github.com/GaryFigno/agent-workflow-audit/releases/download/v0.1.0/paper1-jss-manuscript.pdf). Prepared for the Journal of Systems and Software.
This is a manuscript reading copy, not a peer-reviewed or accepted journal article.

The paper reports a retrospective single-site case. The software implements its
measurement checks, and its public examples are synthetic. Slowlife is future work;
no Slowlife observations are present. Reduced production records remain in a separate
replication archive and are not included in the public code repository.
